# High-Risk Transaction Policy
Synthetic portfolio policy, version 1.0. This is not a bank policy or a legal standard.

## 4.2 Enhanced investigation
Transactions with a risk score greater than or equal to 80 require enhanced investigation and escalation for manual review. A score is a model estimate and does not establish fraud. Review amount, account history, balance anomalies and counterparty behavior. Never automatically freeze an account based on this copilot.

## 4.3 Moderate and low scores
Scores from 50 through 79 require manual review. Scores below 50 require standard review; a low score does not establish that a transaction is safe. If policy evidence or model evidence is unavailable, return INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE rather than an approval.
