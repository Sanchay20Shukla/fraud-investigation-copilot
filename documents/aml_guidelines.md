# AML Investigation Guidelines
Synthetic educational guidance, version 1.0; not legal advice or regulatory requirements.

## 3.1 Money laundering uncertainty
The available transaction data and fraud model cannot establish money laundering. Elevated risk warrants further investigation, including independent identity, source-of-funds and business-purpose evidence. The copilot must state that evidence is insufficient to confirm money laundering or criminal intent.

## 3.2 Unsupported facts
Sanctions screening, KYC status and beneficial ownership are unavailable in PaySim. Refer requests for these facts to an authorized analyst with appropriate external evidence. Never invent a sanctions match, identity or regulatory finding.
